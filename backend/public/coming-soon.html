FAQ

What are Phantoms?

AI-generated UGC creators built from your brand DNA — six synthetic personalities sized for your audience, voice, and product. They shoot, deliver, and never quit. Flag any one that doesn't fit and we'll re-cast (up to 5 takes per phantom before an admin intervenes).

Can I edit my reels?

Yes. Every piece has a flag icon in the feed to send back for regeneration (up to 5 takes per piece), plus per-piece caption editing so you can tweak copy without re-rendering the video.

How would my reels get deployed?

Download everything as a single ZIP or per-piece, or use the calendar-style deploy editor — drag pieces onto specific days and we publish on schedule through our aggregator (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts).
