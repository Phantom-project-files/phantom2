Privacy Policy

Effective: 20 June 2026 · Operated by Fallacie Creator Studio LLC ("Phantom", "we", "us")

Phantom builds AI-generated marketing creative for businesses. This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and who we share it with.

1. The short version (data label)

At a glance, here is every category of data we touch:

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your business content to train third-party models beyond what is required to generate your output.

2. How we use your data

  • To run the product: analyze your brand, generate and store your creative, and show it back to you.
  • To secure the service: rate-limit, detect abuse, and debug errors (IP + request logs).
  • To communicate with you about your account and generations.

3. AI processing & disclosure

Phantom's creators ("Phantoms") and the reels and posts they produce are AI-generated. Your brand inputs are sent to our AI sub-processors (below) solely to produce your output. Generated likenesses are synthetic and do not depict real people.

4. Sub-processors

  • Anthropic — AI analysis & copywriting (Claude).
  • Enhancor — AI image & video generation.
  • Cloudflare (R2) — media storage & delivery.
  • Stripe — payments (only once billing is enabled).
  • Ayrshare — social publishing (only once deploy is enabled).

5. Data retention

We keep account and generated content for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward for backups and legal obligations. You may request deletion at any time (see §7).

6. Security

Sessions use opaque, randomly-generated tokens stored server-side; cookies are HttpOnly and (in production) Secure. Secrets are stored outside the codebase. No system is perfectly secure, but we apply reasonable safeguards.

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. To exercise them, contact us at vaibhav@fallacie.com.

8. Cookies & sessions

We use a single session cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

9. Children

Phantom is a business tool not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by a new effective date here.
